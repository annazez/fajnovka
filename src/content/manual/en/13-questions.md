| Question | Answer in one sentence | Where the evidence is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why not simply a cheaper fare? | A discount for all is mainly a subsidy for people who already ride; we pay only for a change, and we keep regular passengers with recognition, for 0.55% of the compensation. | Why it will work: Deutschlandticket |
| How many drivers really change? | An estimated 280–600 a year, by two independent methods; for this reason we build mainly on retention and return. | Business model: How many are truly new |
| What if it does not work? | The pilot costs CZK 3.4 million and has rules for expansion and for the end that are set in advance. | The pilot |
| How do you know that the effect is not chance? | A control district and a randomised experiment, with a number of people that is calculated in advance. | Measurement and fairness |
| Who pays for it? | The city, ideally with the region and KODIS, which get a part of the benefits; the partners carry 85% of the discounts. | Ten years: Wider benefits |
| Why would partners give 50%? | The customer pays the other half and comes into free capacity; we start with city institutions. | The reward system |
| What about GDPR? | Only with consent, only pass purchases, no location, no sale of data. | Measurement and fairness: Fairness rules |
| Why not an application? | The card is enough and an application is optional; seniors and children can take part too. | Why it will work: Beat the Street |
| Will the vehicles not be too full? | About 12% of the capacity is in use in the rush hour, and points are also given for off-peak trips. | The problem |
| Why is the car the competitor, and not another ticket? | Each car in a household makes the next trip by car cheaper; the programme works best together with a parking policy, but it does not depend on it. | The problem: The main competitor is the car |
| Why start with the employees of the city? | The city can decide for itself without a negotiation, it tests the benefit on its own people, and it gets the first data. | How Fajnovka reaches people |
