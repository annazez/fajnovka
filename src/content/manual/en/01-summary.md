Fajnovka is a loyalty programme of the City of Ostrava on the personal ODISka card. It takes the mechanics of supermarkets, airlines and blood donation and uses them in the public interest. Each year, Ostrava loses an estimated 12,000 holders of a long-term pass. For this reason, Fajnovka mainly slows down their departure and brings back those who left. It wins new passengers from cars where this is cheap: at the moments of a life change.

- **Those who stay get recognition.** A level for the years with a pass (bronze, silver, gold, diamond), on the model of the Czech blood donor medals. A higher level gives a better chance in the prize draw, a reward at each move to a higher level, and discounts at partners.
- **Those who leave do not fall at once.** The level fades step by step, with a clear way back. The programme continues to court those who left, according to the reason for their departure.
- **Those who change their behaviour get points.** A newcomer gets up to 700 points for the first steps (registration, first month, three months, first annual pass). Points are exchanged for 50% discounts on culture, sport and partner offers.
- **Families and groups** have a shared account. Households that give up their second car get the "One car less" package.

**The result in one sentence:** the programme costs CZK 11.6 million a year (0.55% of the city's compensation to DPO). In the base scenario it pays back to the city budget in 2034, and it brings about CZK +256 million to the whole region in 10 years. In the pessimistic scenario it does not pay back to the city budget. For this reason, it starts with a pilot for CZK 3.4 million, with a stop rule that is set in advance.

**What this document does not claim:** that Ostrava, DPO or KODIS prepare a similar system. All parameters of the programme are a proposal for verification. Where a value is an estimate, the text says so.
