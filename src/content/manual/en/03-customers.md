The customer does not buy "green transport". The customer buys a calmer morning, money saved on a car, and experiences with the family. The city and the partners pay for the programme. The user pays nothing extra.

| Archetype | Who | The problem in their words | What Fajnovka offers | When to speak to them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Nováks, a family with two cars | Martin 41, Jana 38, children 7 and 11 | "In the morning we drive the children, in the afternoon the clubs. We cannot do it without the second car." | shared account, "One car less" package, family rewards | September (first year of school); when the second car needs an expensive repair or a roadworthiness test |
| Petra, commutes by car | 38, free parking at work | "By car I am there in 15 minutes and I do not have to change." | trial month without commitment, company challenge | January; a change of parking at work |

| Klára, student | 19, technical university, uses public transport | "Now I take the tram, but when I work, I want a car." | Colours of Ostrava and concerts for points, competition of faculties | Colours of Ostrava; end of studies and first job |

The archetypes are models, not data. They need verification in interviews with 5–8 people from each group.

### Where to aim and with which data

The annual reports of DPO do not show passengers by sex, age, education or place of residence. Targeting is therefore possible by place, life event and fare group. KODIS knows sex, age and place of residence from the personal ODISka cards. With consent and in aggregate, these are the best source, and they belong in the data request to KODIS.

| Source | What it contains | Use for targeting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profiles on the ODISka (KODIS) | age category (child, student up to 26, adult, senior), history of passes | levels, life cycle, moments such as the end of studies |
| O2 mobile data / MAPPA (2022) | more than 100,000 regular commuters, more than 200,000 less regular ones; flows between districts | which corridors and districts |
| Czech Statistical Office and the fajnOVA analysis | 51% of the people who move out of Ostrava stay in its hinterland | a move out of the city as a moment of outflow |
| Shared bikes (Transport Service Plan 2026–2032) | more than 94,000 registered users; a holder of an ODISka has 30 minutes for free | cross-selling of bike + public transport |
| fajnOVA survey 2023 (5,190 respondents) | satisfaction with public transport 66%, with parking only 31% | message to drivers: parking is their pain |

**Priorities of targeting:**

1. **Corridors Jih – Moravská Ostrava, Poruba – Moravská Ostrava and Jih – Poruba.** Public transport is strongest there, and free capacity is cheapest. Pilot district: Jih or Poruba.
2. **Life events:** end of secondary school (driving licence, first car), end of studies, parents of first-year pupils, a move out of the city, start of a new job.
3. **Commuters from the surroundings:** park and ride, the regional ODIS pass, company challenges at large employers. Their revenue goes to KODIS, so it is not in the DPO model.
4. **Users of shared bikes** and **drivers who suffer from parking** (centre, hospitals, universities).
