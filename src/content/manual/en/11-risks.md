title: Risks
order: 11
---

| Risk | Probability | Effect | What we do about it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ODIS cannot give the data about passes to the points account | medium | high | the first question for KODIS; a pilot with an export from the ODIS e-shop and a pairing of cards |
| The churn of regular passengers does not change | medium | high | the pilot measures it; the stop rule |
| Partners do not accept discounts with a contribution of 15% | medium | medium | start with city institutions (free capacity), add private partners after the first results |
| Low registration | medium | medium | a level from the history at once after registration; registration at a ticket office and on the website |
| GDPR and consent to data processing | low | high | consent at registration, a data protection impact assessment (DPIA), only pass purchases, no location |
| Deadweight | high | low | large points only for a change; recognition, not points, for regular passengers |
| Cheating | low | low | daily caps, points only for paid trips, a check of anomalies |
| Financing after an election or a change of priorities | medium | medium | a small pilot, shared financing with the region and KODIS, which get a part of the benefits |
| Vehicles too full in the rush hour | low | medium | about 12% of the capacity is in use; points for off-peak trips |
