### The psychology of rewards

| Mechanism | What research says | In Fajnovka |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A goal within reach | Customers of a café loyalty programme bought more often when they were nearer to the reward (Kivetz et al., 2006). | The account shows how many points are missing; the first reward comes within 2 weeks. |
| A head start | A card with two of ten stamps already filled works better than an empty card with eight fields (Nunes and Drèze, 2006). | Welcome points; a progress ring that is "already half full". |
| Steps of products | Alfred Sloan of GM offered "a car for every purse and purpose": from Chevrolet to Cadillac. | The customer always sees the next step: month, three months, year, family. |
| Status and its loss | A demotion hurts more than a promotion pleases; demoted customers were less loyal (Wagner et al., 2009). | The years with a pass never expire; only the active level fades. |
| A threshold and public recognition | Blood donors gave more often before they reached the threshold of a medal, and did not give less after it (Lacetera and Macis, 2010). | Levels for 1, 3, 5 and 10 years. |
| A streak | A broken streak lowers motivation, but less when it can be repaired (Silverman and Barasch, 2023). | A counter of months in a row, one repair of the streak each year. |
| A random reward | A small chance of a big prize motivates more than a sure small gift; Singapore (INSINC) offered a game for a variable reward instead of credit. | A prize draw by level, without paid chances. |
| A window of change | A move and parenthood go together with a change in transport use (Whittle et al., 2022); the decision starts about 2 months before the change (Cape Town). | Double points for new employees, new residents and first-year students. |
| A fresh start | People start new goals after milestones: New Year, the start of a month or a semester (Dai, Milkman, Riis, 2014); but an announced milestone tempts people to delay (Koo et al.). | Campaigns in January and September; announce bonuses only on the start day. |
| Social influence | A meta-analysis confirms the strength of social incentives for green behaviour (Nguyen-Van et al., 2021). | Team challenges of companies, schools and districts. |
| Loyalty programmes | A meta-analysis of 40 years of loyalty programmes finds a small but positive effect on loyalty (Belli et al., 2022). | The model counts with a small effect: churn lower by 0 to 1.5 percentage points. |

**Risks and how to handle them:**

- **Return to the old habit when rewards end.** In the Dutch Spitsmijden project, rewards cut rush-hour car trips by about 60%. When they ended, the drivers came back. Fajnovka therefore runs permanently and builds on habit and identity, not on a short campaign.
- **Deadweight.** The people who already ride collect the most points. A Swiss randomised experiment (Sticher et al., 2024) did not measure a significant effect of money rewards or social norms. Therefore large points go only to change, and regular passengers get recognition, not points for trips.
- **Crowding out of inner motivation.** The evidence is mixed: a natural experiment shows the opposite effect too (Wollbrant et al., 2022), and a meta-analysis of 16 field experiments (Vorlaufer et al., 2025) did not find crowding out after payments ended. Rewards are therefore experiences and discounts, not money, and the reason is communicated.
- **Cheating.** At Citi Bike, users moved bikes back and forth only for points. Fajnovka has daily caps and gives points for a trip only when the trip is paid.

### Models

Cities have already tried points for sustainable transport. But no programme has a control group, so nobody knows how many trips would happen without the points. Fajnovka is to be the first to correct this.

| Programme | Where and when | Points for | Reward | What was found |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Bella Mossa](https://cbsm.com/cases/34117-bologna-s-bella-mossa) | Bologna, from 2017 | walking, bike, public transport; max. 4 trips a day | discounts at 85–100 local shops | 15,000 people and 895,000 trips in 6 months; no control group |
| [Kultur-Token](https://digitales.wien.gv.at/en/?p=4862) | Vienna, test from 2020 | trips without a car (CO2 saved) | tickets to cultural institutions | covid stopped the test with 1,000 people; the nearest model for rewards in culture |
| [INSINC / Travel Smart](https://landtransportguru.net/travel-smart-journeys-schemes/) | Singapore, 2012–2019 | trips outside the morning rush hour | credit, or a game for a variable reward | a successor has run since 2020 |
| [Beat the Street](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9594084/) | Hounslow (London), 2019 | a card touched to readers | points and prizes | 28,219 players in 6 weeks; works without a phone |
| [S ODISkou na hrad](https://www.muzeumct.cz/images/muzeum_v_mediich/Cervenec_2023/Jabko_7-2024.pdf) | Moravian-Silesian Region | a personal ODISka card | 50% discount at 6 castles and chateaux | about 2,000 uses in the first year; the ODISka already is a discount card |
| [ČD Body](https://www.skrblik.cz/cestovani/jizdenky/vernostni-program-ceskych-drah-cd-body) | Czech Railways, from 2014 | ticket purchases | tickets and culture | a Czech model for the exchange of points for transport and culture |
| [Bruxell'Air](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/services-et-demandes/primes-et-aides-financieres/la-prime-mobilite-bruxellair-pour-se-deplacer-autrement-bruxelles) | Brussels | the deregistration of a car | mobility budget of €566–1,132 | [more than 1,200 premiums in 2023](https://bx1.be/categories/news/la-prime-bruxellair-augmente-a-partir-du-1er-mars/); the model for "One car less" |
| [Deutschlandticket](https://www.cleanenergywire.org/news/germanys-flat-rate-transport-ticket-working-mobility-and-climate-govt-evaluation) | Germany, from 2023 | a cheap flat-rate pass | – | [existing subscribers cut car use by only about 7%](https://tum.de/en/news-and-events/all-news/press-releases/details/deutschlandticket-hardly-reduces-use-of-cars); a discount works for new users, for existing ones it is a subsidy |

**Lessons:** rewards in culture cost little when they use free places (Vienna). A card without a phone includes children and seniors (Beat the Street, ODISka). A discount for all is mainly a subsidy for existing users (Deutschlandticket). That is why Fajnovka rewards change.
