**Before the pilot, weeks 1–6:**

- KODIS and DPO: monthly data about renewals and new holders for 2022–2025 (they make the churn and the baseline more exact), the shared account, GDPR, the price list for 2026, the revenue from regional passes.
- The first question for ODIS: does it record the purchases and the history of long-term passes on the card, and can it connect the ODISka cards of a family or a group?
- Partners: 3 city institutions, 2–3 companies, 2 schools in the pilot district, the organiser of Colours of Ostrava; verify the 50% discounts with a contribution of 15%.
- The city as an employer: the benefit for the employees of the City Hall, the city districts and the city organisations.
- The city and the region: their position on the family pass (step B), on "One car less" and on shared financing (KODIS gets a part of the benefit).
- DPO: who takes over the support and the analyses (the customer centre, the data team), and which CRM tool DPO already has.
- Data: households with 2+ cars (census 2021), the occupancy of cultural institutions, the number of users of park and ride.
- 20 interviews across the archetypes; then adjust the points, the rewards and the messages. Ask drivers: "Would you try a month of public transport if the first reward came within 2 weeks?" Ask parents with two cars: "For what would you give up the second car?"

**The pilot, 6 months from September:** a randomised experiment in the window of change, a step-by-step launch by district, and target values of the indicators that are set in advance. At the end, the 10-year plan is calculated again and the city decides: expand, adjust, or end.
