Fajnovka has three layers. **Steps** show where to go next: from a trial month to a family, and from a newcomer to the Diamond level. **Points** are large for change and small for situations. **Rewards** are 50% discounts in the city, a prize draw and recognition. Everything runs on the personal ODISka card, and points come mainly from the purchases of passes that ODIS records.

### Steps of passes

Since December 2022, the DPO tariff has only 30-day and annual long-term passes. The steps therefore stand on them.

| Step | Pass | Points | For whom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Registration | registration of the ODISka in Fajnovka | 100 | everyone |
| 1. Trial | first 30-day pass (or the first after a break of at least 12 months) | 150 | a driver who wants to try public transport without commitment |
| 2. Habit | third 30-day pass without a gap | 150 | those who lasted three months |
| 3. Year | first annual pass | 300 | those who already take public transport as their main way |
| 4. Family | second adult in the household with a long-term pass | see Families and groups | families with children |

Each step is a small commitment, and the next one is always in view. A driver does not have to decide "I will use public transport". "I will try a month" is enough. After the registration and the first step, a newcomer has 250 points, which is the first reward at once. The whole way to an annual pass gives 700 points.

### What gives points

| Behaviour | Points | Cap | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steps 0–3 | 100 / 150 / 150 / 300 | once in 12 months | purchase in ODIS |
| Window of change: new employee of a partner company, new resident, first year of university | points for steps ×2 for 3 months | once | confirmation from the company, office or school |
| Park and ride, then public transport | 10 | once a day | reader at the car park |
| Trip by public transport to a partner event | 20 | twice a week | ticket + valid pass |
| Trip outside the rush hour (bank card, electronic purse) | 2 | 4 trips a day | record of the trip |
| Team challenge of the month (companies, schools, districts) | bonus for the winning team | once a month | sum of the team's points |
| A month with a long-term pass | no points: the month adds to the level | once a month | ODIS |

A regular passenger does not get points for usual trips. This passenger has a level, the prize draw and discounts at partners. Points are given only for a paid trip, so nobody can get them without revenue for DPO. Points are valid for 12 months, with a notice 30 and 7 days before they expire.

### What points buy

Points are not exchanged for free entry, but for **50% discounts**. The person pays the rest, so the partner does not get only a cost, but a paying customer. For the person it is a reward in the full value of the discount; the city pays only a fraction.

| Points | Example of a reward | Why this level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | 50% discount at an outdoor pool or on a child's museum ticket | first reward within 2 weeks of registration |
| 250 | 50% discount at the zoo, a museum or a cinema | goal of the first 3 months |
| 500 | 50% discount at a theatre or a concert | goal of the first year |
| 1,000 | 50% discount on a family day (zoo for 2 + 2, wellness, amusement park) | goal of families |
| any | give points to trees in the city | for those who need nothing |

**Who pays for the discount:** city institutions give it from free capacity, so it is extra income for them. Private partners take it as marketing. The city contributes to partners about 15% of the value of the discount on average (estimate; to agree with partners). **1 point equals about CZK 0.5 of discount**, so one redeemed point costs the city about CZK 0.075.

### Levels: pride, prize draw and benefits

The level says how many years a person has used public transport. The model is the Czech blood donor award: the Czech Red Cross gives [a silver medal for 20 donations, a gold one for 40 and a gold cross for 80](https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/archiv/cesky-cerveny-kriz-v-lounech-ocenil-darce-krve-214555), and the way to gold takes [about ten years](https://www.olomouc.eu/aktualni-informace/aktuality/23212). A level gives three things: a better chance in the prize draw, a reward at the move to a higher level, and discounts at partner businesses.

**Rules of the levels:**

1. **The months with a pass are never deleted.** In a gap, only the active level fades (see Life cycle).
2. **The level is visible** in the account, on the card and in the chance in the prize draw. At the move to a higher one, a badge comes by post.
3. **The top is rare:** diamond for 10 years.
4. **The reward comes at the move, not each year,** so regular passengers do not cost more than they bring.
5. **Partners pay for the partner discounts for level holders,** because the discounts bring them customers.

The prize draw replaces ceremonies: one main prize motivates thousands of people, the level has weight in it, the main prize for ten-year passengers is a goal for years, and the winners are social proof ("a lady from Poruba has used the tram for 12 years, and she won").

**How many people will be at which level (estimate for the first year).** Holders of a long-term pass collect levels, seniors included: about 49,000 members. About 31,850 of them have at least bronze. The other members (occasional passengers, newcomers, holders for less than a year) are "Fajnováci", the base level. Few people get to the top: the history of passes on the card does not always go back 10 years, and there are gaps between the years.

| Level | Condition (months with a pass in total) | Members (estimate) | Share | Moves to the level in a year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fajnovák | registration | 48,560 | 60% | – |
| Bronze | 12 | 14,700 | 18% | 6,100 |
| Silver | 36 (3 years) | 9,800 | 12% | 3,100 |
| Gold | 60 (5 years) | 5,390 | 7% | 1,450 |
| Diamond | 120 (10 years) | 1,960 | 3% | 350 |
| **Total** |  | **80,410** | **100%** | **11,000** |

**The prize draw follows the number of people at a level.** Each level has its own monthly draw, and the rule is **number of prizes = number of members × target chance**. When more people come, more prizes come, and the chance of one person stays the same. One common drum would not work: 48,000 Fajnováci would outvote 2,000 Diamond members.

| Level | Chance to win in a year | Prizes a month | Value of a prize | Nominal value a year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fajnovák | 0.9% (1 in 111) | 38 | CZK 300 | CZK 136,000 |
| Bronze | 1.5% (1 in 67) | 18 | CZK 500 | CZK 110,000 |
| Silver | 2.3% (1 in 44) | 19 | CZK 800 | CZK 180,000 |
| Gold | 4.1% (1 in 24) | 18 | CZK 1,500 | CZK 331,000 |
| Diamond | 11% (1 in 9) | 18 | CZK 3,000 | CZK 647,000 |
| Diamond draw (yearly) | 1 in 980 | 2 main prizes | CZK 100,000 | CZK 200,000 |

Usual prizes are tickets and vouchers from free capacity and from partners. The real cost is half of the nominal value, and about 85% of the prizes are collected. Main prizes are paid in full. The prize draw therefore costs about **CZK 0.8 million a year**.

**Reward at the move to a higher level:**

| Move to | Reward | Moves in a year | Cost for the city at full use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | 200 points | 6,100 | CZK 92,000 |
| Silver | 50% discount on 2 tickets for culture or sport | 3,100 | CZK 140,000 |
| Gold | 50% discount on a family day | 1,450 | CZK 174,000 |
| Diamond | experience package from partners at half price | 350 | CZK 166,000 |
| **Total** |  | **11,000** | **CZK 572,000** |

At a use of 45%, the rewards for moves cost about CZK 0.26 million a year. When the data show a different distribution of members, the numbers of prizes change, not the thresholds of the levels.

### Member life cycle: decline, return, departure

A person who stops riding does not fall down at once. The level fades step by step, and each phase has a clear and easy way back. The principle is the same as in Duolingo, but without punishment: the member gets a specific date, a return with one click, and the option of a pause.

| Phase | When | What happens to the level | What we say | How to return |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before the pass ends | 7 days and 1 day before | nothing | "Your pass ends on Friday. You have been with us for 37 months in a row." | renewal with one click |
| Grace period | day 1–30 | nothing | "You have 30 days, your streak continues." | buy a pass, the streak continues |
| Decline | day 31–90 | the draw is one level lower; partner discounts continue | "Your Diamond chance waits until 15 March." | a return within 90 days restores everything; the streak can be repaired once a year |
| Sleep | day 91–365 | no draw, discounts paused, the months stay | once in 2–3 months, an offer that fits the reason for leaving | return month: double points, the level is active at once |
| Departure | after 12 months | the member has left | win-back strategy (below) | return month, the history counts |

**Pause:** once a year, a member can announce a pause of up to 3 months in advance (parental leave, illness, work abroad). The member loses nothing, and the programme learns the reason.

**Win-back strategy for those who left:**

1. **Find the reason:** on day 30 after the pass ends, one question: "What changed?" (a move, a car, a new job or school, the price, the connections, a pause).
2. **An offer that fits the reason:**
   - *Moved out of the city* (51% of those who move out stay in the hinterland): the regional ODIS pass, park and ride, and points for park and ride.
   - *Bought a car:* "car and public transport": points for park and ride, offers in Advent and during road closures.
   - *New job or school:* a trip planner, a return month, a company challenge.
   - *Price:* the 10+2 programme and an annual pass in instalments (to verify with DPO).
   - *Connections or quality:* pass it on to DPO; write again when something on the route improves.
3. **Contact calendar:** months 1, 3 and 6 after the departure, then in January, in September, on the anniversary of the first pass, and during a closure on the member's route. At most 6 messages a year for 24 months, then once a year, always with consent.
4. **A win-back offer costs at most the value of one month of fares.** A return is the cheapest way to win a passenger (CZK 95 for a passenger-year, see Business model).

**Each type of member has a different start:**

| Type | First 90 days | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completely new to public transport | welcome and 100 points; day 7: tips for the route; first reward within 14 days; day 25: "try one more month"; day 60: family or colleagues | survive the first 3 months, when most people leave |
| Regular passenger, new to Fajnovka | a level from the history at once, the first prize draw, an overview of partner discounts | recognition and a reason to stay, for little money |
| Returning member | "Welcome back, your 52 months are still here"; a return month with double points | prevent a second departure |
| Occasional passenger | Fajnovka passport, events, the draw for Fajnováci; an offer of a trial month | move to the first step |

### Families and groups: the shared account and "One car less"

The start of school is a window of change, and children often have an ODISka: in 2026 the region gives [the first ODISka for free to children born in 2019 and 2020](https://www.zaben.cz/evt_file.php?file=20330), and seniors from 65 travel in Ostrava [for CZK 0](https://www.arecenze.cz/?p=408518). But the parents often drive, and the household has two cars.

**A family annual pass: yes, but only as the second step.** A person with a prepaid pass travels to work for CZK 0 extra, so the car becomes the more expensive choice. But data from Germany show that this works mainly for new holders. For families that already have passes, a discount would only cut revenue, and the city and KODIS must approve the tariff.

|  | Step A: Shared account (at once, no change of tariff) | Step B: Family annual pass (a political decision) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | the ODISka cards of parents and children in one account with common points | a lower price of the annual pass for the second adult in a household with a child up to 15 |
| What the family gets | +300 points when the second adult first enters the steps; +500 points a year when both parents have an annual pass | a direct discount (for example a half; only an example for the calculation) |
| Deadweight | low, points are only for change | high, families that already ride get the discount too |
| What it needs | to connect ODISka cards in an account (to verify with KODIS) | a change of the ODIS tariff and a calculation of the effect on revenue |

Step A shows how many families respond to the offer. Only with these data does it make sense to negotiate step B.

**Group Fajnovka** (proposal for the pilot; not in the cost model yet). The shared account does not have to stop at the family. People know the idea from family plans of music and video services: one plan, several people, each with their own profile. A group of two to six people who live or commute together (a couple, flatmates, colleagues from one office) can connect their ODISka cards in one account. The points are common, the levels stay personal, and the group gets the same bonus as a family when one more adult enters the steps. The group brings what an advert cannot: a person you know who already rides. The pilot must show two things: whether groups bring truly new passengers, and how to stop groups that exist only for the bonus (a cap on bonuses, and points only for a change).

**The "One car less" package** (on the model of Bruxell'Air in Brussels), for households with two or more cars:

- **Condition:** the household permanently deregisters or sells a car that it had for at least 12 months, and does not register another one for the next 12 months. The proof is an extract from the vehicle register.
- **Reward:** an annual pass for one adult and 1,000 points, nominally about CZK 4,900 for a household. Brussels gives €566–1,132. Start with 100 households a year, extend by interest.
- **Support:** a consultation "how to manage without the second car".
- **Honestly:** a sold car does not have to leave the city. The measure is therefore the trips of the household, not the number of cars in the city. How many households in Ostrava have 2+ cars must be found (census 2021).
