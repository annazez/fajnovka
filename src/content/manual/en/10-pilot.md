---

The pilot verifies the two numbers on which the programme stands: if levels and win-back messages decrease churn, and if the welcome offer in the window of change brings new passengers. The rest ("One car less", the family pass, wide campaigns) comes after it.

- **Where:** Poruba (about 60,000 residents, an estimated 18,000 holders of a long-term pass). The control district is Ostrava-Jih.
- **When:** September to February (6 months), with preparation from the spring. The measurement of renewals continues to month 12.
- **What runs:** registration, the steps, levels from the history, the prize draw, the member life cycle and win-back messages, the welcome offer as a randomised experiment, 3 cultural partners, 2 schools, 2 companies. We propose that the first employer in the pilot is the city itself (see How Fajnovka reaches people).
- **A fast signal:** the monthly renewals of 30-day passes show a change of churn after only 3 months. The yearly renewals show it after a year.

| Item of the pilot | CZK million |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum IT: export of ODIS data, a points account on the website, e-mails, the prize draw | 1.00 |
| Team for 6 months (head, marketing and CRM, partners part-time) | 1.16 |
| Rewards: discounts, the prize draw, levels for about 12,000 members | 0.15 |
| Campaign in the district | 0.30 |
| Evaluation: design of the experiment, questionnaires, analysis | 0.35 |
| Legal and GDPR | 0.15 |
| Reserve 10% | 0.31 |
| **Total** | **3.42** |

The pilot fits the budget that the challenge of the city expects (CZK 1.5–3.5 million).

**The decision after the pilot (the rules are set in advance):**

- **Expand to the whole city** if the churn in Poruba is lower than in Jih with a probability of at least 80% (a Bayesian evaluation of the monthly renewals; the pilot reliably sees an effect from 1.5 points, a smaller effect needs the whole city) and the welcome offer increases the conversion by at least 3 points.
- **Adjust** if only one of the two works: expand only the part that works (loyalty and returns, or the welcome of new passengers).
- **End** if neither works. The loss is limited to the price of the pilot.
